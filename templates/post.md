Start with the problem you ran into. What made it interesting enough to investigate?

## What I tried

Describe the smallest useful experiment. Include the code or measurements a reader needs to follow it.

## What changed my mind

Explain a surprise, a limitation, or a trade-off. Distinguish what you measured from what you suspect.

## Where this leaves things

End with what you would do next, or what a reader can use today.
